# PRISMHORN

**You never cast a spell — you cast a rainbow.**

A free browser game. One shaft of dawn strikes your horn and leaves it as seven
bands of light. Each band is a verb. They fire **together, always, in a rigid
fan** — so every shot you take is seven decisions at once, and you aim the whole
spectrum or none of it.

Built for **js13kGames 2026**, theme *"Unicorns and Rainbows"*. The entire game —
art, sound, save system and all — is **13,312 bytes zipped**, which is smaller
than the icon on most storefronts.

---

## Play

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **WASD / Arrows** | walk the valley |
| **Q / E** | narrow ‹ › widen the fan |
| **SPACE** | flare — the bands reach further and hit harder, paid out of vigour |
| **ENTER** | walk in, and walk back in |
| **1–6** at the Prism | spend Light |
| **SHIFT+R** | begin again from wave 1 (you will not need this) |

## The seven bands

The order is spectral and it never changes. A band always leaves your horn from
its own slot, whether or not you have unsealed it — so buying one changes
geometry you have *already learned* instead of just adding a number.

| | band | what it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ■ | **UNMAKE** | dissolves the made |
| ■ | **FORGE** | hardens into matter |
| ■ | **WAKE** | animates the dormant |
| ■ | **GROW** | accelerates living things |
| ■ | **CHILL** | slows and stills |
| ■ | **SCRY** | reveals what is hidden |
| ■ | **MEND** | restores what it touches |

**The trap is in the table, not in the difficulty.** GROW is your entire economy:
it ripens the blooms that pay you in Light. It is also the fastest way to breed a
monster, because the blight standing next to that bloom is a living thing too, and
GROW does not know the difference. MEND heals your cairns and it heals whatever is
eating them. FORGE hardens your ground and it armours the thing on it.

You cannot fire one band. That is the game.

## Falling is not losing

Run out of vigour and the light goes out — and you keep **every** upgrade, every
band, and every mote of Light you were carrying. You return to the Prism and walk
back into the same valley. There is no run to lose and nothing to grind back.

Your progress is saved in your browser automatically.
